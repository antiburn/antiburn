# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want participation in antiburn to be safe, respectful, and productive.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Give clear and useful technical feedback.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Focus disagreement on the work, not the person.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect the privacy of contributors and users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private session content, or other sensitive user data.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person represents the project in
public. Project spaces include the repository, issue tracker, reviews, and other
official communication channels.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through the project's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/antiburn/antiburn/security/advisories/new).
The maintainers will review reports promptly and protect the reporter's privacy
as far as practical.

Maintainers can edit or remove contributions and can temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. They will apply actions according to the seriousness,
frequency, and impact of the behavior.
